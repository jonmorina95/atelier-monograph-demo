Villa Limestone

VAL D'ORCIA, TUSCANY

Excavated from a single travertine seam. The interior living volume is treated as a continuous negative relief, illuminated by a deep zenithal slot window tracking diurnal solar movement.

Casa Sculptura

BASEL, SWITZERLAND

A vertical core carved from polished basalt with integrated bronze channel handrails. Serving as both structural spinal shear wall and acoustic damper across four split-level atelier floors.
