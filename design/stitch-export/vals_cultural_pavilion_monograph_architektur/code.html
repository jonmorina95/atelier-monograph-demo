MATERIAL SYSTEM

RAW GNEISS & BLACKENED ZINC

The exterior envelopment is comprised of board-formed concrete incorporating locally quarried Valser quartzite sand and river gravel aggregates. The timber formwork boards were rough-sawn larch from the immediate valley, transferring distinct organic wood grain into the mineral facade.

Secondary volumes and roof caps utilize custom 1.2mm standing-seam blackened zinc plates, weathered via patination baths to an austere slate tone that absorbs fluctuating alpine light rather than reflecting it.

FIG. 03.1 — ZENITHAL SKYLIGHT DETAIL LEVEL 0

A 24-meter narrow ceiling slit delivers directional zenithal light onto monolithic limestone partitions, rendering subtle textural gradations across the day.

FIG. 03.2 — BASALT STAIRWELL DESCENT LEVEL -1

Monolithic basalt treads cantilevered from lime plaster retaining walls conduct visitors downward toward the submerged acoustic chamber.
